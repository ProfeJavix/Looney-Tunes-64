-Bugs Bunny: He jumps higher (naturally) than the others, and hovers on his triple jump with his long ears spinning like a helicopter.
-Daffy Duck: He runs fast and moves in a very "bouncing off the wall" type of fashion (I imagine he would have a Pac-Man-esque ground pound where he bounces off the ground when he does it). He also have a big wooden mallet he can use in place of punching. He also could hover
-Porky Pig: Fairly basic, he's sort of the all-around character (might not even need a moveset).
-Elmer Fudd: He is also fairly an all-around type, but he wields a shotgun most commonly in the shorts, so if possible, he'd use it to shoot enemies and breakable objects rather than using his fists.
-Sylvester: Since my friend, Tilly, is modelling him to have Tweety representing his "cap," he'd have a sort of Odyssey-esque moveset, in that Tweety would be a sort of projectile who can also carry Sylvester in a brief hover.
-Marvin the Martian: He'd wield a laser gun (much like Elmer's shotgun) and potentially throw explosive devices (he's all about his "earth shattering kabooms").
-Taz: He's very melee heavy, in that he not only punches and kicks, he bites. He COULD potentially eat his enemies and spit them out, OR he could grab them and carry them with ease then throw them if that's more feasible. He'd also have his iconic spinning "tornado" attack (much like Crash Bandicoot's spin move). He's tough as nails, so he takes a bit less damage than others, but he's not as agile.
-Foghorn Leghorn: He's a brawler, and his hits deal a notable amount of higher damage than the others. He could have a charged-up slap attack (much like when he says "ahhhh, shaddup!" in the shorts), which launches the enemy fairly far (more so than a kick would).
-Lola Bunny: Imagine Bugs's moveset, but Lola is lighter on her feet (faster runner) and can hover longer than Bugs. There's a BIT of Daffy's moveset in there too, in that she's a more chaotic type of character (not necessarily including a mallet).
-Yosemite Sam: Like Elmer and Marvin, he uses a weapon to shoot enemies (two pistols specifically), and he COULD have a shoulder bash to ram through enemies and break through objects such as brick walls. I'm also considering having him use his pistols as a mid air boost that not only heightens his jump but also shoots below him onto who or whatever is in his line of fire.
-Wile E. Coyote: Perhaps the most interesting moveset of all, he can briefly speed through the air on an Acme Rocket (a la the classic shorts), and if he runs off a ledge, he can keep running (or hovering technically) in the air for a few seconds before realizing he's not on the ground anymore and falling down.
